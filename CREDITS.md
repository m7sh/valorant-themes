- Collected agent wallpapers supplied by the bundle owner.
- Official agent renders used to composite the branded signature wallpapers are
  served by the community [valorant-api.com](https://valorant-api.com/) project.
- Signature wallpapers are composited locally from those renders with ability-colour
  gradients. They are not official Riot wallpapers.
